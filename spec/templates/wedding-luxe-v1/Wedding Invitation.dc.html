Our story

June 2019 · Lisbon

The meeting

Two strangers, one hopelessly wrong map, and the last two seats at a tiny fado bar in Alfama. Amara asked for directions; Julian gave the wrong ones on purpose.

March 2022 · London

The little flat on Rowan Lane

One key, two toothbrushes, and a kitchen far too small for two people who both insist on cooking. We kept the flat. We kept each other.

September 2025 · The Dolomites

The question

At sunrise above Lago di Braies, Julian finally gave the right directions — down on one knee. She said yes before he finished the sentence.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I bring a plus one?+

If your invitation says “and guest,” absolutely. Otherwise we’ve kept the day intimate — thank you for understanding.

Are children welcome?+

We love your little ones, but the evening is adults-only. Babes in arms are always welcome at the ceremony.

What about gifts?+

Your presence is the present. If you insist, a contribution to our honeymoon in Kyoto would mean the world.

Will the ceremony be outdoors?+

Yes, weather permitting — bring a wrap for the evening. If England does its thing, we move to the Orangery, which is arguably prettier.

Can I share photos from the day?+

Please do — after the ceremony. We’re having an unplugged ceremony, then tag everything #TheHartOfIt.
