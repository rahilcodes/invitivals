FAMILY & FRIENDS PEACOCK — CLAUDE ANIMATION ASSET PACK

VISUAL SOURCE OF TRUTH
Use 01_REFERENCE/finished_reference.png for the final composition, scale, colors and spacing.
The invitation should remain elegant and visually continuous with the previous engagement screens.

TEXT
For production, recreate these words as HTML/CSS/SVG:
“With best compliments from”
“Family & Friends”
Use the blank decorative panel as the backing when possible. Do not flatten normal text into the scenery.

RECOMMENDED LAYER ORDER
z0 background landscape / waterfalls / gazebo
z10 architectural arch and columns
z20 balcony/railing and steps
z30 floral swags and hanging strings
z40 decorative text panel
z50 side and foreground floral clusters
z55 peacock
z60 petals / leaves
z70 gold ornaments / hanging gold element
z90 HTML/CSS/SVG text

PEACOCK
Keep the peacock prominent in the lower-right/center foreground, matching the reference.
Recommended motion: extremely subtle breathing/parallax only; optional tiny head movement.
Do NOT make the peacock flap, walk, bounce, or move dramatically.

FLORALS
Top hanging strings: very slow, tiny sway.
Foreground flowers/leaves: extremely subtle parallax.
Avoid excessive motion.

PETALS
Use only a few petals at a time.
Slow downward drift with slight horizontal movement.
Do not create a heavy petal shower.

LANDSCAPE
Keep the waterfall/gazebo scene mostly still.
Optional very subtle waterfall shimmer/flow if performance allows.

ARCHITECTURE
Arch, pillars, balcony and steps remain static.
They visually connect this screen to the surrounding invitation screens.

GOLD ELEMENTS
Use occasional low-intensity glint only.
Hanging ornament may have a tiny pendulum sway.

CONTINUOUS SCROLL
Do not make this section look like a separate poster pasted between screens.
Carry the floral edges, ivory/gold architecture and landscape naturally into adjacent sections.
No white/black flash and no hard cut.

TRANSPARENCY
All individual exports are RGBA PNG files with transparent safety padding.
Inspect on checkerboard/dark grey before deployment.
