TEXT
Do not flatten “Engagement Ceremony” into a decorative PNG. Recreate it in HTML/CSS/SVG so Claude can position and animate it cleanly.

MOTION
Chandelier: tiny slow sway + occasional subtle glint.
Curtains: extremely gentle fabric movement.
Hanging floral strands: 1–2° slow sway.
Flowers/leaves: subtle depth parallax only.
Loose petals: sparse, slow falling animation.
Rings: optional very gentle highlight/glint; do not spin continuously.
Landscape, arch, marble platform: remain still.

CONTINUOUS INVITATION
Keep the next/previous screen preloaded. Maintain the same ivory-gold architecture, burgundy/pink/teal flowers and warm lighting. Avoid hard cuts, black/white flashes and rectangular overlays.

TRANSPARENCY
All individual assets are RGBA PNGs with transparent safety padding. Check against checkerboard/dark grey before production use.
