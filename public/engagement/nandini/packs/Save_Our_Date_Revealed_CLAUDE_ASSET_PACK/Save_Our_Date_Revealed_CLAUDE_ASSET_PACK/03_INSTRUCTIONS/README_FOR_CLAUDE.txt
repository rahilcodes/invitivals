ANIMATION

Envelope body: remain mostly still.
Ribbons: slight loosen and settle.
Wax seal: tiny press/release + subtle glint.
Hanging flowers: slow tiny sway.
Petals: sparse slow fall.
Landscape: static or extremely subtle water shimmer.
Arch/steps/balustrade: static.
