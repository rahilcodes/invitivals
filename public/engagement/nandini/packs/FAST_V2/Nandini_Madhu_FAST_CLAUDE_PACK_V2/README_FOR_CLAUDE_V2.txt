PURPOSE
-------
This is the deadline-friendly version. The finished invitation reference images are the main artwork.
DO NOT rebuild the invitation from these overlay PNGs.

ANIMATION_OVERLAYS contains only a few optional transparent overlays:
- 3 complete hanging lanterns
- 3 complete hanging floral pieces
- 1 rose-petal group
- 1 soft gold glow

USE THEM SPARINGLY.

CLAUDE RULES
------------
1. Keep every finished reference template intact and in its approved composition.
2. Overlay only a FEW of the supplied animation PNGs where they visually fit.
3. Lanterns: very gentle pendulum sway, about 1–2 degrees, slow 4–7 second loop.
4. Hanging florals: very gentle side-to-side sway, about 1–2 degrees, slow 5–8 second loop.
5. Petals: use sparingly. Slow drift/fall; never cover names, date, venue, or buttons.
6. Gold glow: low opacity only for occasional chandelier/gold highlight breathing.
7. DO NOT place every overlay on every page.
8. DO NOT use overlays to hide section joins.
9. DO NOT crop, stretch, zoom, or use object-fit: cover on overlay PNGs.
10. Keep overlay containers overflow: visible.
11. The couple, rings, swans, peacock, architecture and landscapes may remain still.
12. Preserve continuous mobile scroll with no white/black gaps.
13. Keep the opening-door interaction and Save Our Date envelope interaction.
14. The closed/open envelope references are two states of ONE section.
15. Google Maps and Save the Date buttons must remain functional.

PRIORITY
--------
Reference composition first.
Smooth continuous scroll second.
Door/envelope interaction third.
Subtle lantern/floral/petal motion fourth.

If an overlay does not fit naturally, DO NOT USE IT.
A beautiful mostly-static section is better than a misaligned animated section.
