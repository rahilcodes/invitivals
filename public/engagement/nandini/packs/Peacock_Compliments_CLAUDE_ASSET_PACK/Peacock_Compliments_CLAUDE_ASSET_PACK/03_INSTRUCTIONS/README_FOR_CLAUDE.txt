RECOMMENDED MOTION
Peacock: keep mostly still; optional 1–2 px breathing/parallax only.
Top floral garland / hanging strands: very slow 1–2 degree sway.
Leaf sprigs: tiny parallax, 2–4 px maximum.
Petals: sparse slow fall; avoid constant heavy particle effects.
Waterfall: keep image still unless using a very subtle masked shimmer.
Gazebo, arch, pillars, balustrade and steps: remain still.
Flower clusters: extremely subtle depth/parallax only.

POSITIONING
Match finished_reference.png. Keep the peacock in the lower foreground, the waterfall centered behind it, and architecture framing the scene. The ivory text area should remain clean and readable. Recreate “With best compliments from / Family & Friends” with HTML/CSS or SVG, not as a PNG.

CONTINUITY
For a scrollable invitation, preload the next section beneath the current one. Carry the same ivory/gold architecture, burgundy/pink florals, teal accents and warm sunset lighting into adjoining sections. Avoid hard cuts, white flashes, black flashes or rectangular overlays.

QUALITY / TRANSPARENCY
All individual files are RGBA PNGs. Each has a transparent outer safety border. Keep assets at native resolution where possible and do not stretch disproportionately.
